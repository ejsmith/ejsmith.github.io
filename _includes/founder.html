The founder's lens

Twenty years of running the business, not just writing the code.

I bootstrapped two software companies, CodeSmith Tools and Exceptionless, with no outside capital. Over two decades their products brought in more than $16 million, and I was responsible for all of it: the code, pricing, sales, support, servers and payroll. Every technical decision eventually shows up in a support ticket, a hosting bill or a renewal.

Reading between the lines

A feature request is rarely about the feature. Behind "can you add an export button?" there might be a customer who doesn't trust the numbers, a sales deal that's stuck, or a workflow nobody understood.

Running businesses taught me to look at a tiny technical detail, step all the way back to see how it fits the product, the customer and the business, and then come back with the question behind the question. It's the skill I lean on most at Concord.
