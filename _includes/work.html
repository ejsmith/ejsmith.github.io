Today

Replacing a 40-year-old system with a platform.

Distinguished Engineer · Concord Finance · 2023 – now

Concord provides credit servicing and software for specialty finance, with more than $60 billion in assets under administration. I led the greenfield build of Concord Link, the platform replacing a green-screen system that had run the business for more than four decades.

We started with almost no written business requirements, so the job was as much about understanding the business as writing code: learning how servicing really worked from the people doing it, and designing for needs nobody could spell out yet. Rather than build for one lending vertical, we built a platform. Like Salesforce for loan servicing, it adapts to any kind of loan, lease or line of credit, so the business can grow into new markets without starting over.

Concord Link is already live with clients, and in Concord's own words it's built and tested "with AI at every stage of development."

The two systems that made it adaptable

scripting & durable workflows

Business rules as configuration, not code

In loan servicing, every client's contract is a little different. I designed and built Concord Link's scripting engine on Jint so those differences live in configuration: sandboxed JavaScript rules for payment allocation, fees, interest, statements and collections. On top of it I built durable workflows that can wait days for a payment or an account event and then pick up exactly where they left off, with a visual builder so operations staff can design them without writing code.

  • Triggered by account events, schedules or staff actions
  • Durable workflows with suspend, resume and deterministic replay
  • Scenario tests, versioning and AI-assisted authoring
  • Workflow core released as open-source Jint.Workflows

imports & exports

Meet clients where they are

Servicing runs on file formats that haven't changed in decades, and clients weren't going to change them for us. So I designed and built an import and export pipeline that adapts to them: declarative field mappings plus scripted transforms, encrypted files over SFTP or S3, on a schedule. Onboarding a new client and migrating them off the legacy system became setup work instead of new engineering.

  • CSV, Excel, XML and JSON, with PGP encryption
  • Mappings built from a sample file, with live preview
  • Record-level errors, retries, de-duplication and audit trail
  • The repeatable migration path off the legacy system
